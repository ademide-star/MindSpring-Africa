
🧠 MindSpring
Play • Learn • Remember • Protect Your Brain
Overview
MindSpring is a gamified mobile application designed to improve brain health awareness, promote dementia education, stimulate cognitive function, and support caregivers through engaging, evidence-informed digital tools.
Developed as part of the Brain Health Academy Africa – Learning in Action Project, MindSpring combines education, cognitive training, behaviour change, and digital health innovation to encourage healthy ageing and dementia risk reduction in African communities.
